# Kadirur/kathiroor village History 

• Kadirur (also spelled Kathiroor) is a historical census town and Grama Panchayat in the Thalassery taluk of Kannur district, Kerala, India.

## Name Origin 

• Land of the Sun: Local tradition and legends trace the name to Kadiravanur (the settlement of the Sun God, Kadiravan), linked to the ancient Kadirur Surya Narayana Temple.
• Paddy Fields: Another tradition notes that because the area was filled with lush, ripe ears of paddy (Kathir), it became known as Kadirur (the place of the grain ear).

## Ancient & Religious History

• Suryanarayana Temple: The Kadirur Surya Narayana Temple is considered one of the oldest Surya temples in South India, with roots traced back to the 13th century or mythical legends involving Lord Rama and Sage Agastya.
• Mysore Invasion: During the campaigns of Tipu Sultan in 1789, the temple was attacked and left deserted for decades until it was renovated in 1825 by Kappanna Karunakara Menon under British administration. It was later taken over by the HR & CE department in 1973.

## Martial & Feudal Heritage

• Kalaripayattu Lineage: The region has a deep tradition in martial arts, famously associated with figures like Mathiloor Gurukkal and local folklore like the Ponnyath Ankam.
• Feudal Lords: Historically, vast tracts of land were controlled by prominent local janmi families and landlords, such as the Thamprakkal of Ponnuyathakathoottu Mana, Kavunkara Illam, and others who managed tenant farming and local administration.

## Freedom Movement & Modern Era

• National Struggle: Kadirur played a vibrant role in the Indian independence movement. Dr. Rajendra Prasad visited Kathiroor in 1934, and the Kottayam Taluk Congress session was hosted here in Kathiroor Vayal in 1936, anchored by local leaders like Madappally Gopalan and K.K. Kunhikrishnan Adiyodi.
• Panchayat Formation: The Kathiroor Grama Panchayat was officially established in 1942, and organizations like the Kadirur Service Co-Operative Bank (founded in 1956) helped shape its agricultural and economic foundation.

## Normal Climate / Whether in kadirur :

Kadirur (located in the Kannur district of Kerala) has a tropical monsoon climate (Am under the Köppen classification), characterized by high humidity, warm temperatures year-round, and distinct seasonal rainfall patterns.

## Key Climate Characteristics

• Temperature:
	• Average daily highs range around 31°C to 33°C (88°F–91°F), and can peak higher during the summer months.
	• Average nighttime lows stay warm, typically around 24°C to 25°C (75°F–77°F).
    • Humidity:
	• Humidity levels are consistently high throughout the year, frequently staying above 75% to 90%, especially during mornings and the monsoon season.
• Seasons:
	• Southwest & Northeast Monsoons (June to September / October to November): Kadirur receives heavy rainfall during the monsoon months, bringing frequent downpours and thunderstorms.
	• Winter / Pleasant Season (December to February): This period is relatively cooler, drier, and more comfortable, though still warm during midday.
	• Summer (March to May): Hot and very humid, with daytime temperatures peaking and occasional pre-monsoon showers or thunderstorms in the evenings.
    

## Famous personalities of kadirur :
The most famous personalities associated with Kadirur include:

### Martial Arts & Legends -

• THE EPICENTER OF KALARIPAYATTU: Kadirur holds a monumental, century-old status in North Kerala's history as a prominent training hub for Kalaripayattu, the ancient traditional Indian martial art form.
• THE LEGEND OF THACHOLI OTHENAN: In the 16th century, the historic Ezharakandam field (also known as Azharakandam Mythanam) in Ponniam was the site of the legendary "Ankam" (medieval duel) between the famous folkloric hero Thacholi Othenan and his master, Kathirur Gurukkal. The duel ended tragically when Othenan used an unethical combat move called 'Poozhikadakam' (beheading his master) to win. Shortly after, Othenan was shot dead in a revenge assassination by a rival disciple named Mayankutty.
• Kadhirur Gurukkal: A legendary 18th-century martial arts master and expert in Kalaripayattu. He is most famously known in northern Kerala's folklore (Vadakkan Pattukal) for his historic duel against the legendary warrior Thacholi Othenan.
•PONNIATHANKAM FESTIVAL: To commemorate these legendary 16th-century warriors, a massive annual martial arts festival called "Ponniathankam" is organized at the Ezharakandam field every year during the Malayalam month of Kumbham (February–March) by the Kerala Folklore Academy. Recognized as the world's largest Kalaripayattu festival, this 7-day event serves as a major showcase for skilled performance artists. The Kerala state government allocated 8 crores for the preservation and promotion of this heritage site.


### Literature & Academia -

• V.V.K. (Valiya Veetil Kunnikannan Gurukkal) (1091–1962): A widely celebrated poet and Malayalam teacher at Kadirur High School. His famous works include Suvarna Meghala, Hridayagayakan, and Nirvana Mandalam.
• K.V. Manan Gurukkal (1890–1965): Born in Kadirur, he was a distinguished Malayalam professor at Government Brennen College. He is remembered for his anthology of poems Divakara Chinta and his essay collection Sameeksha Soudham.

### Politics & Public Service -

• P. Jayarajan: A prominent political leader from Kerala who has served as a Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) and held key leadership positions within the CPI(M) in Kannur.
• Chathukutty Nair: A highly respected early local leader who served as the President of the Kadirur Panchayat.

### Cinema (Associated Figures) -

Sreenivasan: While born in the neighboring village of Patyam, the legendary Malayalam film actor, screenwriter, and director completed his pivotal early education at Government High School, Kadirur.

## Temples in Kadirur:

### Kadirur Sree Suryanarayana Temple
	• Deity: Lord Surya Narayana (combined form of the Sun God and Lord Vishnu, holding Shankha, Chakra, Gada, and Padma).
	• Significance: One of India’s oldest and second-largest Surya temples (after the Konark Sun Temple), derived from Kadiravapuram ("land of the Sun").
	• Legend: Associated with Lord Rama, who consecrated the idol either after chanting the Aditya Hridayamantram or in response to the meditation of local Vedic Brahmins. Features traditional mural paintings and darushilpam (wooden sculptures).
	• Timings: 05:00 AM – 11:00 AM and 05:00 PM – 08:00 PM.
    
Key Offerings (Vazhipadus) for Sree Suryanarayana temple - 
      
• The primary and traditional offerings (vazhipadus) at the Kadirur Sree Suryanarayana Temple (located near Thalassery in Kannur, Kerala) include:

• Neyyamrithu: Pure ghee offered in a small pot to the deity (a prominent signature offering).
• Neyvilakku & Niravilaakku: Ghee lamps lit for the deity.
• Pushpanjali & Sahasranama Pushpanjali: Flower offerings chanted with divine names.
• Payasam Varieties: Palpayasam, Sharkkara payasam, and Neypayasam.
• Nivedyams & Edible Offerings: Aval Nivedyam, Vella Nivedyam, Appam, and Thrimadhuram.
• Chandanacharthal & Kalabham: Sandalwood paste and special kalabham or alankara deepam.
• Udayasthamana Pooja & Visheshal Pooja: Comprehensive daily or special ritual poojas.
• Special Rituals:
	• Swayamvara Pooja: Held on Mondays for removing obstacles to marriage.
	• Navagraha Pooja: Held on the second Saturday of every month to ease planetary doshas and improve Surya Dasha.

Offerings for Upa-Devatas (Sub-Deities)-

• Sree Ganapathi: Ashtadravya Ganapathi Homam, Otta Appam, and Thenga Muttal.
• Sree Devi: Devi Pooja and Thrikala Pooja.
• Sree Paramasivan (Shiva): Jaladhara and Dravyabhishekam.
• Homam: Maha Mruthyumjaya Homam and Karuka Homam.

## Kadirur / Kathiroor panchayat office :

### Kadirur panchayat overview 

Kadirur Gram Panchayat is a body of elected representatives of the people to manage the administrative affairs of 
the Panchayat Raj institutions. For a Gram Panchayat, it is the main body that implements the decisions of 
the Gram Sabha and carries out the day-to-day administration. The main components of the Panchayat are: 
President: He is the head of the Panchayat. He presides over the meetings and supervises the overall activities 
of the Panchayat. Vice President: He assumes the duties of the President in his absence. Ward Members: Representatives 
elected from each ward of the Panchayat.

### Vision and Mission 

The vision of Kathiroor Grama Panchayat is to grow into a hygienic and healthy village with the aim of people's welfare and 
comprehensive development. The main objective is to move forward with the participation of all sections based on social justice,
environmental protection and sustainable development. The mission of the panchayat is to ensure the best infrastructure,
education, healthcare, drinking water and sanitation for the people and to give priority to women empowerment, 
youth development and promotion of agriculture. The mission of the panchayat is to elevate Kathiroor as a model 
panchayat by ensuring public participation and providing transparent governance and efficient services.

### About kadirur village office  
Kathirur derives its name from the word Kathirur, which means the Ur of Kathir, as it is a land rich in paddy fields. 
The match between Mathilur Guru and Thacholi Othenan took place at Kathirur village.
The gold medal is very famous. History has it that during the British rule, the village courts of Kathirur were 
located on the Kachcheri Param where the present Village Office is located. At that time, the power to collect taxes 
in Kathirur was given to the Kookkal family. For about thirty years the tax was levied by the Komappan authority of the 
Kookal clan. After his death, the village was reorganized on 1.9.1961 and the existing Ponniam section was merged with 
the Kathirur section to form the present Kathirur Village.

Tourist places in Kadirur :

Kadirur is more renowned for its rich historical, cultural, and martial arts heritage rather than conventional commercial 
tourist spots. However, it holds immense significance for history enthusiasts, culture lovers, and pilgrims. 

Key places of interest and heritage sites in Kadirur include -

Kadirur Sree Suryanarayana Temple: This is one of the oldest and second-largest Surya (Sun) temples in South India, 
with roots dating back to the 13th century or mythical legends involving Lord Rama. 
It features traditional mural paintings, wooden sculptures (darushilpam), and unique historical architecture.

Ezharakandam Field (Ponniam): Famous as the historic site of the legendary 16th-century medieval duel ("Ankam") between
 the martial arts master Kathirur Gurukkal and the folkloric hero Thacholi Othenan. 
 It is also the venue for the massive annual "Ponniathankam" Kalaripayattu festival organized by the Kerala 
 Folklore Academy, which is the world's largest martial arts festival.

Traditional Sacred Groves (Kavus) and Temples: Places like the Chozhan Rayaroth Sree Payyamvelli Temple and local
 sacred groves offer visitors a chance to witness traditional and vibrant Theyyam performances, 
 especially between December and March.

Natural Pools and Scenic Spots: Natural swimming locations like the 6th Mile Natural Pool, 
Kadirur Chira, and the Ponniam West Kodangat Swimming Pond offer a glimpse of the region's serene landscape 
and natural water bodies.


Festivals & Cultural Events in Kadirur :

• Ponnyathankam (Kalari Festival)
	• When: Annually in late February (e.g., February 21–27).
	• Where: Ponniam Ezharakandam / Ponniam Vayal (near Kadirur/Ponniam).
	• Details: Celebrated as a massive Kalaripayattu and folk martial arts festival, this event commemorates the legendary 16th-century historic duel (Ankam) between Thacholi Othenan and Kadirur Gurukkal. Top Kalaripayattu performers from across the region gather to exhibit their skills. Read more on Manorama Online.
• Pulliyod Sri Kurumba Bhagavathi Temple Festival
	• When: Annually in March (typically 3 days, around March 11–13).
	• Where: Pulliode, Kadirur, Thalassery.
	• Details: One of the largest local community festivals in the area, drawing massive crowds for traditional temple rituals and celebrations.
• Theyyam and Kavu Festivals
	• When: Peak season runs from December to April, with specific local kavu celebrations occurring on dates like October (e.g., Thacholi Muthappan Madappura and surrounding shrines in Kadirur) and March at sites like Manoli Kavu (Vadakkumbad).
	• Where: Various sacred groves (kavus) and shrines across Kadirur and Thalassery (including Kakkara Antholikkavu and local madappuras).
	• Details: Features sacred ritual dance forms like Muthappan Vellattam, Gulikan, and various Bhagavathy Theyyams with intricate facial cosmetics and heavy headdresses.
• Kodiyettu Mahotsavam at Suryanarayana Temple
	• When: Annually in February–March.
	• Where: Suryanarayana Temple, Tellicherry-Coorg Road, Kadirur.
	• Details: A traditional temple annual festival featuring special poojas and community festivities.

Cultural /  Dance / Traditions   in kadirur :

Theyyam Performances: Kadirur and its local sacred groves (Kavus)—such as the Puthiyaveettil Puthusseri Bhagavathy Kshethram—host traditional Theyyam rituals. This sacred dance features artists in vibrant costumes, painted faces, and elaborate headdresses, typically peaking between December and March. Specific Theyyams performed here include Porkkali Bhagavathy Theyyam, Sasthappan (Kuttichathan) Theyyam, and Thekkan Gulikan.
Kalaripayattu & Martial Heritage: Kadirur is historically celebrated as the home of Mathiloor Gurukkal (also known as Kadirur Gurukkal), a legendary grandmaster of Kalaripayattu, Kerala's ancient martial art. The region is also tied to the folklore of the Ponnyath Ankam duel and stories associated with Thacholi Othenan. Local traditional training centers and remembrance events keep this martial legacy alive.
Poorakkali & Ritual Dances: Temple festivals in the region frequently feature Poorakkali, a traditional ritualistic and scholarly dance performed by men in Bhagavathy temples, especially during annual temple celebrations.
Vadakkan Pattukal (Northern Ballads): The cultural fabric of Kadirur is steeped in Vadakkan Pattukal, the classic heroic ballads of Malabar that recount the valor of historical warriors and martial legends of North Kerala.
Temple Festivals & Local Celebrations: Central sacred spots like the Kadirur Surya Narayana Temple and surrounding village temples serve as the focal point for annual temple utsavams, traditional music (such as Chenda Melam), and community gatherings.

Rivers in kadirur :

Kadirur (located in the Thalassery taluk of Kannur district, Kerala) is primarily sustained by the Anjarakandy River system and its associated local tributaries.
The primary waterways flowing through and around Kadirur include:
• Anjarakandy River (Anjarakandy Puzha): This is the prominent river system serving the region. It flows near Kadirur's boundaries and serves as a major ecological and agricultural lifeline for the surrounding plains.
• Kuyyali River / Koduvally River: A key local tidal river and tributary system that flows through the immediate Thalassery-Kadirur vicinity. Notable nearby spots include the Bypass Road Kuyyali River View Point.
• Eranholi River: A closely tied distributary branch of the Anjarakandy River system that runs just west of Kadirur. It features the popular Eranholi River Front walkway.
Additionally, the area features the historical Kadirur Chira, a prominent local wetland and water reservoir system used extensively for local conservation and irrigation.
